Thank you for contributing to uDeck. This agreement sets out the terms under
which your contribution is accepted. It is deliberately short, and it is written
to be read rather than skipped.

documentation, or anything else — you agree to the following.

**In one sentence:** you keep the copyright in what you wrote, and you give the
project owner a licence broad enough to keep distributing it and, if it ever
becomes necessary, to release it under a different licence.
